Schema & Entity Markup Auditor

The idea

The idea is to compare structured data with visible page evidence and identify safer, more connected markup recommendations.

The problem it addresses

Schema often contains missing requirements, disconnected entities, or unsupported assumptions.

Who it helps

Technical SEO practitioners reviewing entity relationships and structured data.

What you can do

Extracts visible signals and JSON-LD, identifies issues, and produces evidence-first recommendations.

The intended outcome

Validate page-level structured data and recommend safer connected markup.
